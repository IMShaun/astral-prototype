# Presentation pages

Recipes from the IMSERV Brand Book Presentation Deck in Figma. File key `z9X7jBOKBvLiSEICk9y3e5`, page `30:536`. Designed by pony.studio. Ask Adrian Cox before you use assets. The official PowerPoint still sits on that Figma page and on Brand Hub with Camilla.

Open `index.html` through the desk preview (`./preview.sh`, then `/assets/brand/pages/`). Pick a recipe. Type over the placeholders. Present is fullscreen. Print this page saves a 16:9 PDF. Do not invent a margin, grid, or colour the book has not shown.

## What the book named

Every slide is **1920 × 1080**. Inset is **40px**. Gutter between columns is **40px**. The cover grid is **128px**. Type is Roobert Medium, Semibold only for small labels. Pairings stay black on white, white on black, cyan on black, and black on cyan. Do not put cyan type on white.

Four-up columns sit at 40, 510, 980, and 1450, each 430 × 860. Three-up columns sit at 40, 667, and 1293, each 586 wide. A split panel starts at 980.

Odd and even frames in a row are twins of the same layout. Ink is black with cyan or white type. Paper is white with black type. Cyan is cyan with black type. Photo sits photography under the 128px grid.

Imagery and Tables 7 is empty in the file. Titles 1 to 6 are not on this page. A sibling Figma page holds PDF Templates. Do not invent A4 margins from this deck. Letterheads stay on their own page.
