# Work Desk transcripts

Turn messy work transcripts into the morning board in `data/board.json`.

The board is split by team (`imserv`, `off-axis`, `stagerise`). Fold talks into that team's board, not into another team.

## When to use

- The user pastes a transcript
- Files appear in `inbox/`
- They ask what they can do today, what they cannot, or what to do next
- They mention IMSERV, Off Axis, Stagerise, user groups, research, strategy, analytics, insights, workflow, access, logins, UI library, language, patterns, skills, branding, prototype, tokens, or the side menu

## How to fold a transcript

1. Read `data/board.json` first. Keep existing Done items for that team.
2. Decide which team the talk belongs to. Default to the current `activeTeam` if the user does not say.
3. Read new files in `inbox/`, or the pasted text.
4. Rewrite that team's briefing for a UX/UI reader who is coming in cold.
5. Save the board, then move processed inbox files to `archive/`.

## Use other skills

Load these skills when the job they name is in the talk. Do not invent a job they do not name.

- Board copy follows `~/.cursor/skills/shaun-writing-skill/` (Shaun writing). No colons, semicolons, or em dashes in board text.
- Product words, labels, empty lines, errors, and help follow `.cursor/skills/ux-writing/` (`/ux-writing`).
- Reusable recipes follow `.cursor/skills/ux-patterns/` (`/ux-patterns`). Inspect first. Recommend one change. Wait for Yes. Run `node .cursor/skills/ux-patterns/scripts/check-library.mjs` when the job is library drift or a duplicate control.
- Walk studies, journeys, and KEIs follow `.cursor/skills/ux-research/` (`/ux-research`). Evidence first. Wait for Build plan.
- Figma library work follows `.cursor/skills/figma/` (`/figma`). Live Astral first. Inventory, map, construct, then validate. Validation measures icon size, type size, Roobert, placeholder copy, and placeholder marks against the live control.

When the user asks to go through Work Desk chrome, Skills, or desk cards, use Astral builders in `astral-ui.js`. Tag, Count, and Button sit on Skills. Folds follow Access. Cards follow Patterns.

## Board rules

Write for a busy scan. Plain words. Short points. No jargon, no client brand voice.

Update these fields on **that team's** board every time

- `headline` One sentence on what matters now.
- `situation` Two short sentences on the current picture.
- `can` Up to three things they are free to do.
- `cannot` Up to three hard limits or not-yets.
- `next` Three concrete next steps, in order.
- `know` Up to three cards. Each has `title` and one-sentence `body`.
- Strategy is not a stored list. The Strategy page reads `next`, `know`, `cannot`, Critical and High insights, and open questions on groups. Keep those fields true. Do not invent a next step a talk has not named.
- Analytics reads `docs/research/experience/keis.json`. `how` is the measurement taxonomy. `structure` is product-wide vs area, and primary vs diagnostic. `cuts` is how a later slice works. `keis` are named scores. A KEI is a score of something specific, important, and actionable. Do not invent a score, a target, or a trend. A small qualitative round is directional only. Walk findings stay in Research.
- `today` Open tasks. They sit in To do list on Today. Tabs are Admin, UI Design, UX Design, Research, and Done. Do not duplicate a task that is already there or in `done`.
- `done` Finished work, newest first. It sits on the Done tab in To do list, not as a side page. Never delete history unless asked.
- `groups` User groups in development. Merge into existing groups. Do not invent a group a talk has not named.
- `research` Walk findings for that team. `insights` are named problems. Do not invent an insight a talk has not named.
- `access` Tools that belong to this team only. Shared company tools and people live on `store.access`, not here.

Company-wide access lives on the root `access` object

- `access.company` Tools shared across teams. Each item needs `id`, `name`, `have`, `ask`, and `lost`.
- `access.people` Who can restore a tool or a file. Each person needs `id`, `name`, `helps`, and `how`.

Never store passwords, keys, or recovery codes.

Each task needs `id`, `title`, `note`, `project`, `kind`, and `added` (`YYYY-MM-DD`). Checked tasks also need `doneOn`.

`kind` is one of `admin`, `ui`, `ux`, or `research`.

- `admin` Access, logins, timesheets, holidays, booking, and other office work
- `ui` Making screens, components, Figma, and vibe coding
- `ux` Behaviours, patterns, journeys, and talks about how it should work
- `research` Looking, watching, reading, and walking a live system

Each group needs `id`, `name`, `status` (`draft`, `working`, or `firm`), `who`, `need`, `heard` (a quote from a talk), `from` (which talk), `open` (one unanswered question), `notes` (`text` plus `added`), and `journey`.

`journey` is an array of stages. Each stage needs `stage` (a short name such as Arrive, See, Notice, Act), `now` (what exists or is known), and `later` (the useful next line, including a prompt if the talk named one). Use an empty string for `later` when the talk has not named that future. Do not invent a later prompt. The Journeys page renders one map per group that has stages.

`research.insights` are named problems from a walk. Each needs `id`, `title`, `problem`, `where`, `why`, `note`, `severity` (`critical`, `high`, `medium-high`, or `medium`), and `score` (1 to 99). `example` is optional. Score ranks within a severity from what the talk named. Harm to access, customer data, or scale sits above extra effort. Critical first, then High, Medium high, and Medium. Use Critical only when a talk named it. Do not invent a red. Do not invent an insight a talk has not named. The Research page is insights only. Insights sit as cards. Switch to a list. Filter by All, then each severity. Walk work still to do goes on Today. Score is not a desk input.

Set `updated` to today.

## What to pull out

From each transcript, keep only what a designer needs

- What exists now, and what is still a prototype
- What they may change vs what they must not touch
- Decisions already made
- Open questions that block UI work
- The next useful action, not a dump of the whole chat
- Who showed up in the talk. Fold them into `groups`
- How a named user moves through the product now, and any later prompt the talk named. Fold that into `journey`
- Tools, logins, or people who can restore access. Fold them into Access. Never copy a password
- Product words, labels, empty lines, errors, and help. Fold them into Language. Do not invent a line
- A reusable recipe for the new product. Fold it into Patterns. Later stays empty unless the talk named it
- A named problem from a walk. Fold it into Research insights. A still-to-do from that walk goes on Today. Do not invent an insight

For user groups

- Only add a group if the talk named them or a real task made them visible
- Prefer a short quote in `heard` over a summary
- Append notes. Do not wipe notes that are already there
- Leave `status` as `draft` until more than one talk backs the group
- Do not invent promoters, venues, or extra roles to fill a set of three
- Company types stay Customer, Broker / TPI, Supplier, and Platform admin. Hats on a Customer stay Energy, Finance, Asset, and Data. Company type is a heading on User groups, not a card. For access

- Only add a tool or a person a talk has named, or that Shaun already holds
- Put shared tools on `access.company` and team-only tools on that team's `access`
- Put restore people on `access.people`
- Never copy a password, key, or recovery code
- Do not invent IT contacts to fill the page
- The Access page folds Who can help, Company, and This team. They start closed. Opening one closes the others. Click again to hide. Shaun adds a card through the Add modal. - Research is insights. Walk work still to do goes on Today.
