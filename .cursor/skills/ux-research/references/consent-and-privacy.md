# Consent and privacy

Never invent legal or privacy terms. Use the project’s approved wording. If none exists, produce a **neutral draft labelled as needing legal review**, and stop short of sending it.

Informed consent should cover, where relevant:

- who is undertaking the research
- purpose
- what data is collected
- recording
- sharing and observers
- withdrawal
- retention
- other processing organisations

Retain evidence of consent associated with each participant’s data.

Use participant IDs (`P01`, `P02`). Do not expose identifiable information in analysis outputs unless the project explicitly permits it.

Respect consent scope, retention, withdrawal, sensitive information, access controls, and sharing permissions. Do not use evidence outside the consented purpose.

## Sharing tiers

| Tier | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Internal raw evidence | Restricted |
| Internal anonymised research | Shareable with product team |
| Stakeholder deck | Anonymised |
| External / public | Only explicitly permitted evidence |

Quotes in decks need consent for anonymised quotation. If unknown, do not include the quote.

Outputs should remove direct identifiers and use anonymised extracts wherever possible.
