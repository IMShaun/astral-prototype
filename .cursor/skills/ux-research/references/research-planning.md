# Research planning

House plan shape (not a rigid template). Omit sections that add no value.

```
Background
↓
High-level objectives
↓
Audience / recruitment
↓
Method / logistics
↓
Preamble and consent
↓
Discovery
↓
Area-specific objectives
↓
Tasks / stimuli
↓
Follow-up questions
↓
Measures
↓
Closing comparison
```

Move from general perceptions and previous behaviour into specific propositions or product areas. Attach explicit objectives to each section. Use interactive ratings only where they serve an objective.

## After Build plan, consider

- Research title
- Research background
- Decision to inform
- What we already know
- Assumptions
- Knowledge gaps
- High-level objectives
- Specific objectives
- Research questions
- Method and rationale
- Participant profiles
- Recruitment criteria
- Exclusions
- Accessibility considerations
- Sample rationale
- Format
- Session length
- Schedule
- Research materials
- Consent / privacy requirements
- Preamble
- Discovery / context
- Research activities
- Usability tasks or interview sections
- Neutral follow-up probes
- Measurements
- Closing questions
- Analysis approach
- Expected outputs
- Risks / limitations
- Objective-to-evidence traceability matrix

## Traceability matrix

| Objective | Research question | Activity | Evidence | Measure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Understand company hierarchy | How do admins understand Company vs Team? | Mental-model questions | Explanation + behaviour | comprehension |
| Evaluate creation flow | Can users add a company? | Usability task | observed task | success + SEQ |
| Understand confidence | Do users know what happens after creation? | post-task probe | quote / rating | confidence |

This lets analysis later ask: **Did this research actually answer the objectives?**
