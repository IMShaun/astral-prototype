# Evidence model

Keep these separate. Do not collapse them into one field called “insight”.

```
RAW EVIDENCE
What happened / what was said
↓
OBSERVATION
A relevant piece of behaviour or feedback
↓
FINDING
What appears to be happening across evidence
↓
THEME
A recurring pattern of meaning
↓
INSIGHT
Why this matters / what it tells us about users
↓
OPPORTUNITY / RECOMMENDATION
What we should investigate or change
```

Capture what was actually observed or said first. Group observations. Determine findings. Only then decide actions. Separate observation from interpretation. Check recordings for verbatim quotes.

## Quote provenance (non-negotiable)

```
Participant: P07
Study: Company Management R02
Session: 07
Timestamp or transcript lines: 00:18:42–00:18:51
Quote: [verbatim text]
Theme: Access confidence
Consent for anonymised quotation: Yes
```

Never create a cleaner-sounding quote. A paraphrase must say `Paraphrase of P07` and must not use quotation marks.

## Insight evidence block

```
Evidence
P03 — “I’d want to know exactly what I'm about to download.”
P05 — “I don't know whether that's all meters or just these ones.”
P08 — paused before Export and returned to filters.

Observed in
3 of 8 participants

Evidence links
P03 00:31:24
P05 00:27:11
P08 00:34:02
```

That is useful. “Users find exporting confusing.” is not.

Reports distinguish **fact / observation**, **interpretation**, **hypothesis**, and **recommendation**. Do not present hypotheses or preferences as findings.
