# Recruitment

Who participates must match the decision. Company admins cannot stand in for Broker / TPI viewers.

## Specify

- Role and organisation type
- Relevant behaviours (manages more than one customer company, exports large portfolios)
- Experience with the current or previous tools
- Exclusions (internal employees when testing customers, people who designed the flow)
- Accessibility needs to plan for
- Sample rationale (why this number and mix)

Qualitative: typically 4–8 per round. Quantitative prevalence: much larger. Do not mix those claims.

## Screener

Ask behavioural questions, not “Are you a power user?” Confirm they actually do the work under study.

Never leave fake names, emails, or companies in a final artefact. Use placeholders such as `[Participant name]` until live details exist.

Screening data is identifiable. Keep it out of shareable decks. Use P01, P02 in analysis outputs unless the project explicitly permits otherwise.
