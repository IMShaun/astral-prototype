# Insight writing

An insight is independent of a single study. The same insight may accumulate evidence over several rounds.

Before creating a new insight, search the repository. Prefer **update existing** over duplicate.

## Format

```
INSIGHT

Concise evidence-based statement.

WHAT WE OBSERVED
Summary of behaviour/evidence.

WHY IT MATTERS
Interpretation.

WHO / CONTEXT
Relevant archetype, role, or situation.

EVIDENCE
Participant IDs and source material.

CONTRADICTORY EVIDENCE
Where relevant.

CONFIDENCE
Low / Medium / High

RELATED AREAS
Features / journey stages / product areas.

OPPORTUNITY
Potential implication. Not an automatic design solution.
```

Confidence reflects evidence quality, breadth, recurrence, and consistency, **not AI certainty**.

Statuses: Emerging, Supported, Strong, Mixed, Superseded, Stale.

Track: first observed, last validated, studies, participants, evidence count, contradictions.

Do not invent walk-severity colours for the desk. Desk `research.insights` stay named findings from talks. Formal study insights live in `docs/research/insights/`.
