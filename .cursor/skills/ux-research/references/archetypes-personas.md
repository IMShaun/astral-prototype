# Archetypes and personas

**Behaviours before demographics.** Archetypes are the primary user model. Personas are an optional presentation layer when useful.

Do not create:

```
Sarah
42
Lives in Manchester
Likes coffee and Netflix
```

Do create behavioural models grounded in research.

## Maturity

```
Candidate behaviour
↓
Emerging archetype
↓
Validated archetype
↓
Established archetype
```

One interview is never enough for a company-wide persona. Do not create a validated archetype from one participant or one weak piece of evidence. Research before attempting to create them.

Demographics only when evidence shows they materially affect behaviour.

Never invent decorative biographies, hobbies, fake quotes, personality traits, or demographics unless supported and relevant.

## Archetype fields

- Name (behavioural, not a fake person)
- Core behaviour
- Primary goal
- Context
- Behaviours
- Needs
- Strategies
- Pain points
- Triggers
- Decision factors
- Relevant journey stages
- Relevant product areas
- Representative **anonymised** quotes (verbatim, with provenance)
- Research coverage, studies, participant coverage
- Contradictory evidence
- Confidence
- Last validated

The Portfolio Controller is an **example shape**, not an invented Astral archetype. Do not add it to `docs/research/` unless evidence supports it.
