# Thematic analysis

Identify and interpret **patterned meaning** across qualitative data. Recursive, not a keyword cluster.

A **theme** has a coherent central organising concept. It is not a topic bucket. Weak: “Reports”. Better: “Users trade flexibility for confidence when reports become consequential.”

```
Transcript ingestion
↓
Evidence segmentation
↓
Objective-aligned coding
+ inductive open coding
↓
Candidate patterns
↓
Contradiction / outlier search
↓
Candidate themes
↓
Theme coherence review
↓
Evidence verification
↓
Insights
↓
Opportunities / recommendations
```

## Steps

1. **Familiarisation** — complete dataset and objectives
2. **Segmentation** — meaningful stretches of evidence
3. **Deductive coding** — against stated research questions
4. **Inductive coding** — unexpected behaviours, needs, concerns
5. **Pattern identification**
6. **Contradiction search** — outliers, minority behaviour, evidence that challenges the dominant reading
7. **Theme construction**
8. **Theme review** — does supporting evidence cohere?
9. **Evidence verification** — every significant claim against source
10. **Insight creation** — what, why it matters, who, context, evidence, what remains uncertain

## Three passes

**Analyst** — codes, patterns, themes, candidate insights.  
**Challenger** — contradictions, alternative explanations, over-generalisation, missing context, assumptions.  
**Verifier** — participant numbers, quotations, transcript references, frequency claims, metrics, evidence links.

Do not publish until Verifier succeeds. Run quote and evidence scripts when files exist.

Frequency is not importance. Allow severe outliers.
