# Interviewing

Use when the question is why people behave as they do, what they currently do, or how they understand a concept.

## Structure

1. Preamble and consent
2. Discovery: current world, last time they did the work, tools, constraints
3. Area-specific objectives
4. Stimuli only after context (prototype, concept, or policy)
5. Comparative or closing questions

## Question craft

Prefer:

- “Tell me about the last time you did this.”
- “What do you think is happening here?”
- “What would you expect to happen next?”
- “What are you looking for?”
- “What would you do next?”
- “What, if anything, is unclear?”
- “What makes you say that?”

Avoid leading, loaded, or double-barrelled questions. Avoid “Do you like this?” without understanding why. Avoid asking people to predict future behaviour when you can observe a task.

Do not mechanically ask every probe on every topic.
