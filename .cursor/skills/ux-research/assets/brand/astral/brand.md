# Astral / IMSERV brand for research artefacts

Do not invent a logo, palette, typeface, or chart style.

## Source of truth

Load **IMSERV Brand Book** values from `data/design-systems.json` (`systems.imserv.branding` and tokens). Work Desk rules already name:

- IMSERV Cyan, Infra Grey, black, white, Roobert
- WCAG AA pairings: black on white, white on black, cyan on black, black on cyan
- Do not put cyan type on white
- Quiet outlines 1px Infra Grey B
- Focus 2px black edge
- Status colour still waits. Do not invent a red for research charts either unless the Brand Book names one
- Product words from Language and `.cursor/skills/ux-writing/references/terminology.md`

## Add these files when you have them

Place real assets here. Until they exist, labelled **neutral draft** only for visual decks.

- Official IMSERV mark (SVG or PNG). Research covers use the black mark on light, as auth screens do.
- Research plan template (document)
- Presentation template (cover, insight slide, quote treatment, table, footer, confidentiality marker)
- Chart treatment (axis, grid, no decorative 3D)

## Tone

Research artefacts are work-facing. No Uber, no Base Gallery, no desk names in participant materials. No We Make Music Work brand. Client-specific studies use that client’s pack, not this folder.

## Confidentiality
