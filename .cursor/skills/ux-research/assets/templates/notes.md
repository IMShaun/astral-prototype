# Observer and notes

## Observer guidance

- Cameras off, mics muted unless invited
- Do not help the participant
- Capture behaviour and verbatim, not solutions
- Flag timestamps for later

## Note-taking

| Time | Task / question | Observation (what happened or was said) | Interpretation (optional, labelled) | Objective |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  |  |  |  |  |

Keep observation and interpretation in separate columns.
