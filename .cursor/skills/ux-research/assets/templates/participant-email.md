# Participant emails

Populate from project metadata. Never leave fake participant details in a final artefact.

Fields: Name, Date, Time, Duration, Study, Platform, Joining method, Preparation, Consent requirements, Researcher, Contact.

## Invitation

```markdown
Subject: Invitation to [study], [duration]

Hello [name],

We would like you to take part in research about [context in plain language].

When: [date], [time], [timezone]
Length: [duration]
How to join: [arrangement]
Before we start: [reading or none]
Consent: [what we will ask]

[Researcher name]
[Contact]
```

## Confirmation

Restate when, length, join details, consent, and how to reschedule.

## Reminder

Send with join details only. Do not add new study content.

## Cancellation / reschedule

Offer a new slot. Do not imply they must continue.

## Thank you

Thank them. Do not include findings. Do not share other participants’ data.
