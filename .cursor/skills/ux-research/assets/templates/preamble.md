# Session preamble

Reusable block. Swap in the project’s **approved** consent wording. Never invent legal terms.

Cover, where relevant:

- introduction
- researcher / company role
- study purpose
- confirmation of participant understanding
- consent
- recording
- observers
- privacy
- voluntary participation and withdrawal
- reassurance that the **participant is not being tested**
- session duration
- note-taking
- moderator time management
- questions before starting

Draft shape (replace with approved copy):

```markdown
Thank you for joining.

I am [name] from [organisation]. I did not build this product. I am here to learn how this work is for you.

We are looking at [purpose in plain language]. There are no right answers. If something is unclear, that is useful.

The session is about [duration]. You can stop or skip a question at any time.

We would like to [record / take notes / have observers: names or “product colleagues on mute”].

We will [how data is stored, who sees it, how long, how to withdraw].

Do you have any questions before we start?
Do you agree to take part on that basis?
```

If approved wording does not exist, label this **neutral draft, needs legal review** and do not send it to participants.
