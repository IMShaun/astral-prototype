# James product walkthrough

James designed the flows and much of the UI at Astral Tech without a dedicated UX person. Function first. The old admin is a monolith with IMSERV, Flow Gas, Shell, BP, EDF, and True Power each wanting different things. Not one shared database. Own imports. Own colours. Own features on and off.

That is why a new customer portal was started. Customers, brokers, TPIs, and suppliers log in there and see about a third of the left nav. Agents stay on the old admin. James does not expect that to change soon.

Shape of the customer portal. Company, then site, then MPAN. Meter level stays hidden. Widgets drag, they do not resize. DataVision makes you find a client first, then a card with stats and a table, then a graph page.

James's unfinished map. A dashboard of widgets. A details page whose core cards stay on. A tabular report without the tree. A modal. Vertical was portfolio, onboarding, maintenance. Horizontal was jump from a widget into site details. Queries inbox is with Clive.

Figma has a component library. James can share it. He sent a Teams video of Alexi restyling the portal in about an hour. Colours are paint. The working build is Angular. React is a live question for Clive, not a fact that the house must be demolished.
