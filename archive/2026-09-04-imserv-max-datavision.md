# Max, DataVision

DataVision lets someone pick sites and meters, compare them to each other or to a fixed number, and export. It always opens on a dashboard. Interval reports are the hard screen. Huge portfolios fall over.

A site is an address. Usually one MPAN and one meter. A tower can have a fiscal meter plus submeters. Do not add both into the site total unless a flag says to. Sometimes people do want both counted. Meter channels sit under the MPAN.

Someone higher in the chain can look down. The lower customer cannot look up or sideways.

Brokers are introducers or framework partners. A letter of authority in sales makes the link. DataVision never checks it. Astral will. Day one can warn that a letter is lapsing. No upload until a later partner portal.

Two jobs. Clockwork reports and alerts, already in Astral. Ad hoc compare and export, still clumsy.

When a visualisation contract is sold, Astral should create the records itself. Historic data stays after the appointment ends.

DataVision was cobbled from factory software. Useful underneath. Poor to use. Lean on James for generic setups.
