# DataVision usability walk with Max

Max walked DataVision as a usability session. He is a super admin, so it opens on which client to impersonate. He used EDF looking at Network Rail. There is no combined EDF portfolio. Hunt is client by client.

Two factor is annoying to him. Veronique and her team live in this system. Bevis Rotherham knows Meterpower and TMA. Max was allotted more to finance than data visualisation.

Interval reports are the real job. He ignores a mostly blank dashboard. IMSERV masters site names. Import is the default. Export is named on the site. DataVision splits one physical site into two rows with two MPANs. A meter can count both ways. Channel would say import or export. Astral’s tree has no equivalent icons or naming yet.

Graphs need an explicit empty when the date range has no data. CSV from the front end is local time. The store is UTC. Export values are positive. You only know export from the site name.

You cannot save a subset of MPANs on the interval screen. Scheduled reports can. Alerts are panic thresholds. You cannot amend an alert. You delete it and start again. Dashboard widgets often show nothing. Site profile, hours, and time of use feel tucked away. Users section is the customer super user adding people.

Version one does not edit address and names. Down the line, operations sit at meter point. Do not copy that into day one.
